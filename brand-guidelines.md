# Athishay Jain — Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0

---

# Brand Purpose

To build products that solve real-world problems by combining engineering, design, and technology into simple, practical experiences.

---

# Brand Positioning

**Product Builder**

Not

❌ Flutter Developer

❌ Web Developer

❌ Embedded Engineer

Because those are only tools.

The real identity is:

> Product Builder who combines hardware, software, design and branding.

# Brand Voice

Writing should be

Simple.

Short.

Confident.

Never exaggerated.

Never buzzword-heavy.

Never try to sound "smart."

Example

Instead of

"We leverage cutting-edge technologies..."

Say

"I build products that solve real problems."

Minimal.

Lots of whitespace.

Large typography.

Rounded corners.

Soft shadows.

No unnecessary gradients.

No glassmorphism everywhere.

No random animations.
