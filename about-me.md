# Master Portfolio Profile
## About Me

# My Journey

My interest in technology began when I was around 10 years old, but it truly became a passion after joining my Diploma in Electrical & Electronics Engineering.

During my diploma, our college organized a project-building competition. That experience changed the way I looked at engineering. Instead of only learning concepts, I realized how exciting it was to build something that solved a real problem.

Another thing that constantly motivates me is curiosity. Whenever I see someone with a skill I don't have, I don't feel discouraged—I feel inspired. My first thought is always:

*"If they can learn it, why can't I?"*

That mindset has pushed me to explore electronics, embedded systems, software development, UI/UX design, branding, photography, video editing, and product development.

Today, I enjoy creating complete products by combining multiple disciplines rather than limiting myself to just one.

---

# Why Electrical & Electronics Engineering?

Electrical Engineering is my foundation and my core strength.

Software development is my passion.

Instead of choosing one over the other, I enjoy combining both to create intelligent products that connect hardware, software, and user experience into a single solution.

---

# My First Major Project

The first project that truly challenged me was **Smart IoT UPS**.

It was my first large-scale embedded systems project involving electronics, IoT, cloud connectivity, sensors, mobile applications, and electrical protection systems.

That project taught me that real learning happens while building.

---

# What Motivates Me

I love solving practical problems.

I enjoy learning new technologies, experimenting with ideas, and understanding how things work rather than simply using them.

For me, engineering is about creating products that improve people's lives—not just completing projects for academic purposes.

---

# My Approach

I prefer building products that have practical value.

Rather than creating projects just to add them to my resume, I focus on solving real problems and building solutions that people can actually use.
