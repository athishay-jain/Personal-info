# Long-Term Vision

My dream is to build a technology startup that creates consumer electronics products capable of solving problems for millions of people.

I want to design products that are practical, thoughtfully engineered, and accessible—products that improve everyday life through a combination of hardware, software, and great user experience.

Rather than focusing on a single product, I want to build a company known for creating meaningful technology that people trust and enjoy using.

# Problems I Want to Solve

I don't want to limit myself to one industry.

Instead, I want to identify meaningful problems wherever they exist and build products that offer simple, effective, and scalable solutions.

For me, the impact of the solution matters more than the industry it belongs to.
