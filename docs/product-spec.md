# Athishay.dev — Product Specification

Version: 1.0

---

# Vision

Create a world-class personal website that tells the complete story of who Athishay Jain is—not just what technologies he knows.

The website should feel less like a portfolio and more like a carefully designed product experience.

It should communicate credibility, curiosity, engineering excellence, and product thinking.

---

# Primary Goal

When someone leaves the website, they should know:

• Who Athishay Jain is.

• What he builds.

• How he thinks.

• Why he builds.

• What he is capable of.

• Where he is going.

---

# Success Criteria

A visitor should be able to answer these questions within 5 minutes.

✓ What kind of engineer is he?

✓ Can he build my product?

✓ Can I trust him?

✓ What makes him different?

✓ What has he built?

✓ What does he want to build?

# Brand Identity

Athishay is not presented as:

❌ Flutter Developer

❌ Web Developer

❌ Embedded Engineer

Instead:

✅ Product Builder

A multidisciplinary engineer who combines hardware, software, design, branding, and engineering into complete products.

• Trust

• Professionalism

• Attention to detail

• Premium

• Calm

# UX Principles

Who are you?

↓

What do you build?

How do you build?

# Content Philosophy

Show.

Don't tell.

Instead of saying:

"I am hardworking."

Show:

30+ IoT Projects

100+ STEM Kits

50+ IoT Products

Real photographs.

Real deployments.

Real products.

---

# Motion Philosophy

Motion exists to improve understanding.

Never to impress.

Animations should guide attention—not steal it.
