# How I Work

## My Process

Every project I build follows the same philosophy.

### 1. Understand the Problem

I don't start by choosing a technology.

I first understand the actual problem.

The better I understand the problem, the better the solution becomes.

---

### 2. Research

Before writing code or designing hardware, I research existing solutions.

I ask questions like:

- Why does this problem exist?
- How are others solving it?
- Can it be made simpler?
- Can it be made more affordable?
- Can the user experience be improved?

---

### 3. Prototype Quickly

I believe ideas should be tested as early as possible.

Whether it's an ESP32 prototype, a Flutter application, or a website, I build an initial working version quickly to validate the concept.

---

### 4. Improve Through Feedback

The first version is never the final version.

I continuously improve products based on testing, feedback, and real-world usage.

---

### 5. Focus on Details

Small details matter.

A clean interface, good typography, intuitive controls, thoughtful animations, proper documentation, and polished product images all contribute to a better experience.

---

## My Design Philosophy

Technology should feel simple.

Users shouldn't need to read a manual to understand how a product works.

Good design removes complexity instead of adding it.

---

## My Engineering Philosophy

A product is more than hardware.

A product is more than software.

A great product combines:

- Engineering
- Design
- Branding
- User Experience
- Reliability
- Documentation

Every part matters.

---

## Continuous Learning

Technology changes constantly.

Instead of trying to know everything, I focus on learning whatever is required to build the next product.

Every project teaches me something new.

That is what keeps engineering exciting.

---

## My Goal

To create products that people genuinely enjoy using because they are simple, reliable, and solve real problems.
